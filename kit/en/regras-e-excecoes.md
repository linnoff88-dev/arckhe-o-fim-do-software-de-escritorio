## Proposed example: restocking inventory

Monitor the risk of a product running out. Check sales, actual stock, supplier lead time, and orders already placed. Prepare a restocking order according to approved criteria. Execute only within an authorized limit and without duplicating an existing order. If data is missing, there is a conflict, or the order exceeds the limit, escalate the case to the responsible person.

The person shifts to negotiating with suppliers, evaluating opportunities, and resolving exceptions. They are also accountable for the delegation criteria.

This example describes a process to be implemented. The kit does not connect to systems, place purchase orders, or execute business decisions. You define the actual values and criteria; the template does not invent a budget.
