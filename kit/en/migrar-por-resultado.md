## Prompt for talking with AI

I want to redesign this process around its outcome, instead of simply producing more documents. Read my map. Identify the goal, necessary context, rules, and exceptions. Propose what AI can monitor, decide, and execute within explicit limits. Separate out work that requires human relationships, creativity, and judgment. Explain what requires real integration and what can be tested manually now. Do not claim to have executed anything if you have only written a proposal. Do not take external actions during this exercise.

## Completion criteria

Someone on the operations team can explain the new workflow, point to a delegable routine, a human-handled exception, and the record that proves execution. This is not an implementation yet. Before delegating real actions, test the workflow and obtain the necessary authorizations.
