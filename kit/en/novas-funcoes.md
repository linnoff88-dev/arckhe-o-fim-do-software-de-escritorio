# Redesign your role: where you can make a difference

Map tasks before discussing job titles. The possibilities below are proposed areas of work, not hiring forecasts or promises of employment.

| Today you spend time on | AI can take on, if prepared | Where you can contribute | What you need to learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consolidating sales in spreadsheets | Retrieve sources, organize metrics, and flag deviations | Understand the deviation with the team and lead the correction | Define metrics, question assumptions, and evaluate outcomes |
| Updating customer records | Connect history, identify signals, and coordinate permitted routines | Deepen relationships, negotiate, and discover new needs | Listening, customer context, engagement criteria |
| Preparing presentations | Choose and produce a format suited to the audience | Create the idea, teach, negotiate, and notice questions | Set an objective and evaluate understanding |
| Following up on tasks and repeating updates | Track records and flag exceptions | Resolve dependencies, conflicts, and priorities | Design processes, define rules and responsibilities |
| Searching for and copying information | Retrieve authorized knowledge and preserve references | Organize knowledge and investigate opportunities | Source quality, access criteria, and analysis |
